Everything the hostel office needs

Four connected modules, one database, real-time status for everyone.

Room Allocation

Students request rooms and track status. Wardens approve, assign, transfer, or vacate — with capacity checks that prevent double-booking.

Complaint Tracking

Maintenance, water, Wi-Fi, cleanliness — submit once, follow every update from Submitted to Resolved, with a full response history.

Student Dashboard

A calm overview of your room, requests, and complaints — plus quick actions to get things done without hunting through menus.

Smart Help

A built-in assistant answers FAQs, explains statuses, and shows your own allocation or complaint status — safely, without exposing anyone else's data.

Built as a complete, working system

This is a college mini-project demonstrating a full stack: an HTML/CSS/JavaScript frontend, a Node.js + Express REST API, PostgreSQL persistence with Prisma, secure cookie-based authentication, role-based access control, and a rule-based help assistant — all working together end to end.

  • Roles for Students, Wardens, and Admins with server-side authorization
  • Transaction-safe room allocation — the last bed can never be double-booked
  • Complaint workflow with student-visible updates and internal staff notes
  • Reports and CSV exports computed from the real database
